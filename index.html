About

Brian Anthony Miller performs as BAM — a Fort Wayne-based stand-up comic known for dark, sharp, and highly observant material. With the polished delivery of a nightside news anchor and the grit of comedy stages, Bam delivers fast punchlines, dry commentary, and local flavor that connects.
